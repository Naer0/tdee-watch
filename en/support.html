Why does the estimate say "holding" – and why does it take time?

The app derives your energy expenditure from two things: how your weight develops over time, and how many calories you ate during that time. Both need time to become meaningful – body weight swings by a kilogram or more from day to day (water, salt, digestion) without any change in body fat. Only the trend over several days tells you something.

"Holding" means: right now, not enough new, usable data is coming in to move the estimate forward – for instance because you haven't weighed yourself for a few days, haven't logged calories, or have marked days as excluded. Rather than pretending the last value is fresh, the app honestly keeps it and tells you it's only being held. As soon as weight and calorie data arrive again, the estimate continues.

In short: weigh yourself regularly (ideally daily, in the morning, after using the bathroom, before breakfast) and log calories without gaps – then the estimate moves too.

How many days of data does the app need?

On day one the app starts with a formula-based estimate from your profile (age, height, sex, current weight). That estimate is gradually replaced by measured reality.

As a rule of thumb: after about two weeks of daily weigh-ins and complete calorie logging, the estimate is already clearly better than any formula; after four to six weeks it is stable. The fewer gaps, the faster. Single missing days are no problem – long gaps or incompletely logged days (the "forgotten" weekend, say) noticeably distort the calculation. You can mark such days as excluded in the app so they don't feed into it.

If you have been logging weight and calories in Apple Health for a while already, the app uses that history too – and gets there correspondingly faster.

Which calorie apps work?

Any app that writes dietary energy to Apple Health. TDEE Watch has no food database of its own and reads calories eaten exclusively from Apple Health – so you can keep using the tracking app you're used to.

To check whether your app writes to Health: Health app → Browse → Nutrition → Dietary Energy → scroll down to "Data Sources & Access". If your tracking app is listed there, you're set. If not, enable the Health connection in your tracking app's settings (usually under "Integrations", "Apple Health" or "Connected apps") and allow it to write Dietary Energy.

The same goes for scales: smart scales that store their weight in Apple Health are picked up automatically. You can also enter your weight directly in TDEE Watch.

Why doesn't the app use activity calories?

Because it doesn't have to – and because it would make the calculation worse.

The energy expenditure TDEE Watch calculates comes from your energy balance: calories eaten versus change in weight. Everything you burn – resting metabolism, daily life, exercise, digestion – is already contained in that balance. Adding a watch's activity number on top would count it twice.

On top of that, wearables' activity calories are notoriously inaccurate for individuals, often by several hundred kilocalories a day. That's why the app deliberately reads no active or resting energy from Apple Health. If you exercise more, you'll still see it – your measured expenditure rises on its own after a few weeks.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

Yes. Under Settings → Units you can switch between metric (kg, cm) and imperial (lb, ft/in); you can also pick this during onboarding. Internally the app always calculates in kilograms and kilocalories, so the result is correct regardless of which unit your scale or tracking app writes to Apple Health.

What does the app cost – and what is free?

Downloading costs nothing. You can go through onboarding and connect Apple Health without paying – after that, a purchase unlocks the app: Today, Plan, History and Settings. There are three ways in: a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription that starts with a free month, and a one-time purchase that lasts indefinitely and never renews.

I deliberately don't quote prices here: they differ by country and can change. What applies in your App Store is shown on the paywall inside the app and on the App Store product page – always in your own currency.

Why a free month rather than a free week: the app needs roughly two weeks of weight and calorie data before it shows a measured expenditure instead of a formula estimate (see "How many days of data does the app need?"). A week would be over before you ever saw the thing the app is built for.

Both subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel. Billing runs through your Apple Account; there is no account with the developer.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by the app – so you cancel in the App Store: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → TDEE Watch → Cancel Subscription. The same screen is where you switch between the monthly and the yearly plan. The app's Settings have a shortcut that opens exactly that screen.

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends, otherwise Apple renews once more. Your access stays until the end of the period you have already paid for. The same goes for the free month on the yearly plan: cancel within it and nothing is charged.

Billing and refunds are Apple's too (reportaproblem.apple.com). I can't see your payments or whether you have cancelled, and I can't change either.

What happens to my data when my subscription ends?

Nothing is lost. When a subscription runs out, the app only locks the view – it deletes nothing. Your profile, plan, plan history, check-ins and settings stay on the device and, if you turned sync on, in your iCloud. Buy again later and you pick up exactly where you left off.

Your weight and calorie data live in Apple Health anyway and belong to you independently of this app: they stay visible there and usable by any other app, even while TDEE Watch is locked.

You can reach your data without buying anything: at the bottom of the paywall there is "Export or delete your data". Behind it sit the same JSON export and the same delete function as in Settings. No purchase and no restore needed.

If you do want your data gone, see "How do I delete my data?" – deleting the app and removing its iCloud data from iOS Settings works whether or not a purchase is active.

I paid and still see the paywall – what now?

Your access belongs to your Apple Account, not to an account with me. So on a new device, after a reinstall, or after switching phones, tap "Restore purchases" – it's on the paywall and in the app's Settings. The app then asks Apple and unlocks whatever is on file for your account.

If the paywall stays, check in this order:

  • Are you signed in to the App Store with the same Apple Account you bought with? (Settings → [your name] → Media & Purchases)
  • Is the device online? Without a connection the app can't confirm the purchase.
  • Is your subscription still running? (Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions)
  • For a shared purchase: is purchase sharing switched on for your family group? See the next question.

If none of that helps, write to me – ideally with the purchase date and Apple's receipt.

Does Family Sharing work?

For the one-time purchase, yes: it is enabled for Family Sharing, so your family group can use the app without paying a second time – provided purchase sharing is switched on for the group. Everyone still works with their own data; what's shared is access, not your profile or your plan.

The two subscriptions can't be shared through Family Sharing; they stay tied to the Apple Account that bought them.

Across your own devices – iPhone and iPad, say – every purchase counts once, as long as both are signed in to the same Apple Account.

How does iCloud sync work – and what isn't synced?

Sync is off by default. You turn it on in the app's settings. After that, the app keeps the following data in sync between your devices via your Apple Account: profile (age, height, sex), current plan and plan history, weekly check-ins, day markings and calculation settings.

Weight and calorie data are not synced. The app reads them directly from Apple Health on each device – and Apple Health has its own sync (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → turn on "Health"). So if weight or calories are missing on your second device, that's down to Health sync, not the app.

The data lives in the private iCloud database of your Apple Account. No one but you – not even the developer – can access it. It takes up a tiny fraction of your iCloud storage. You can turn sync off again at any time; the data on your devices is kept but no longer reconciled.

How do I delete my data?

  • Everything at once: app Settings → "Delete all data". This removes profile, plan, history, check-ins and settings from the device and – if sync is on – the app's data from your iCloud as well.
  • Weights and calories in Apple Health: manage these in the Health app. Weight entries you made in TDEE Watch can also be deleted right in the app. To remove everything the app has written to Health in one go: Health app → profile picture → Apps → TDEE Watch → "Delete All Data from TDEE Watch".
  • iCloud manually: Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → TDEE Watch → Delete Data.
  • Deleting the app: removes all of the app's local data. Whatever is in Apple Health and iCloud stays until you delete it there.

Before deleting, you can export your data from Settings as a JSON file – in case you need it again later.

Does the app work on iPad?

Yes, TDEE Watch is a universal app for iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 26 or later); your purchase covers both, because it belongs to your Apple Account. Note that Apple Health on iPad only shows data if Health sync via iCloud is turned on (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Health). Without that setting, the app sees no weight or calorie data on the iPad.

There is currently no Apple Watch or Mac version.

Why can't I edit some weight entries?

That's an Apple Health rule: an app may only modify or delete the entries it wrote itself. Weights you entered in TDEE Watch can therefore be edited and deleted in the app. Values that come from your scale, another app, or were entered directly in the Health app are displayed by TDEE Watch but can't be changed by it.

Edit those entries at the source – in the Health app (Browse → Body Measurements → Weight → "Show All Data") or in the app that wrote the value. If an outlier is disturbing the calculation but you don't want to delete it, you can simply exclude that day in TDEE Watch instead.

To help me help you quickly, please include:

  • what you expected and what happened instead – a screenshot often helps.

For anything to do with billing, receipts or refunds, Apple will help you faster than I can: payments run entirely through the App Store and I have no access to them. Everything else – features, numbers, bugs – is mine.

Please don't send health data unless it's really needed. And don't worry: I have no access to the data in your app – I only see what you write to me.
