Privacy Policy

for the iOS app TDEE Watch · Last updated: September 17, 2026

In short: TDEE Watch processes your data only on your device – and, if you turn it on, in your own private iCloud. The developer runs no server, has no access to your data, and shares nothing with third parties. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking. Health data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared.

2. What data is processed

TDEE Watch processes only the data needed for the app to work. Which data that is depends on the access you grant.

Health data from Apple Health (only with your permission)
  • Read: body mass (weight) and dietary energy consumed (calories eaten); for the body metrics chart in the history also body fat percentage, body mass index, waist circumference and lean body mass.
  • Write: body mass and waist circumference – only the values you enter in the app yourself.
  • The app reads no other Health data: no active or resting energy, no workouts, no steps, no heart data.
Profile details (entered by you)
Age, height and – optionally – biological sex. They are used for the initial estimate before enough measured data is available, and to calculate body metrics. Optionally also neck, wrist and ankle circumference as well as hip circumferences; Apple Health has no hip circumference type, so the app stores these values itself.
Plan data (created in the app)
Your goal (lose, maintain, gain), the computed calorie budget, plan history, weekly check-ins, days you have marked (e.g. excluded days), and calculation settings.
Derived values
Weight trend, estimated energy expenditure (TDEE) and plan progress. They are computed on your device from the data above.

The app does not process your name, email address (unless you write to us), location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers, device identifiers, usage statistics or crash reports. There is no user account.

3. Purposes and legal bases

Data is processed solely for the following purposes:

  • calculating your weight trend and your true total daily energy expenditure (TDEE);
  • creating and adjusting a calorie budget for your goal, including weekly check-ins;
  • showing your history and progress;
  • saving the weights you enter to Apple Health so they are available there and across your devices;
  • at your request: syncing profile and plan data between your devices via iCloud.

Legal bases:

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (performance of a contract / providing the functions you request) for processing your profile and plan data on the device.
  • Art. 9(2)(a) in conjunction with Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (explicit consent) for health data. You give this consent in the Apple Health permission dialog when setting up the app and can withdraw it at any time (see section 9).
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR (consent) for the optional iCloud sync, which you explicitly turn on in the app's settings and can turn off at any time.

Withdrawing consent does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.

4. Storage location and recipients

On your device. All data is stored locally on your iPhone or iPad (in the app's preferences and in JSON files inside the app's protected storage). It is covered by iOS device protection and – if you use it – is part of your device backup (iCloud Backup or a local backup), which Apple manages under Apple's terms of service.

Optionally in your private iCloud. Only if you turn on sync does the app transfer the data listed in section 6 to the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account. The sole recipient is then Apple, acting as a processor within the meaning of Art. 28 GDPR; the iCloud Terms of Service and Apple's Privacy Policy apply. The developer has no access to the private database.

No sharing with third parties. The developer runs no server of his own, receives none of your data, and passes no data on to third parties – not for advertising, analytics or any other purpose. The developer transfers no data to third countries. Apart from the iCloud sync you request, the app opens no connections to any server.

Purchase via the App Store. Apple handles the purchase of the app. The developer receives no personal data from it, only aggregated sales statistics. Apple's Privacy Policy applies to the purchase.

5. Apple Health (HealthKit)

The app accesses Apple Health only if you allow it in the iOS permission dialog. You can grant or deny reading and writing separately; without Health access the app works only in a limited way, because it then receives no weight or calorie data.

In line with Apple's requirements for HealthKit, the following applies expressly:

  • Health data is never used for advertising, marketing or similar purposes.
  • Health data is not sold, not shared with third parties, and not used for data mining or similar.
  • Health data is not uploaded to iCloud – even when sync is turned on. Syncing your Health data between devices is done solely by Apple Health itself, according to your iOS settings.
  • Health data is used only to provide the features of TDEE Watch described in section 3.

The app writes only weights to Apple Health that you entered in the app yourself. As required by iOS, it can only modify or delete entries it created itself.

6. iCloud sync

iCloud sync is off by default. If you turn it on in the app's settings, the following data is stored in the private CloudKit database of your Apple Account and kept in sync between your devices:

  • profile details (age, height, biological sex, optionally neck, wrist and ankle circumference),
  • hip circumferences entered in the app,
  • your current plan and plan history,
  • weekly check-ins,
  • day markings,
  • calculation settings.

Weight and calorie data are not synced. The app reads them from Apple Health on each device.

The private CloudKit database is part of your iCloud storage and accessible only with your Apple Account. Apple processes this data as a processor; the developer can neither view nor analyse it. You can turn sync off at any time and delete the data stored in iCloud (see section 8).

7. No tracking, no advertising, no analytics

TDEE Watch contains no advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting services, and no third-party software components (SDKs). The app uses no advertising identifier, no cookies and no other identifiers to track you across apps or websites. The developer collects no usage statistics.

This website likewise sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is served by GitHub Pages; when you load it, GitHub (GitHub, Inc.) processes technically necessary connection data such as your IP address under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Retention and deletion

Your data is kept for as long as you want to keep it. There is no automatic deletion period, because the data is available only to you. You delete it yourself:

  • In the app: "Delete all data" in Settings removes all locally stored data and – if enabled – the app's data in your iCloud.
  • Apple Health: manage weight and calorie data in the Health app (Health → Profile → Apps → TDEE Watch → "Delete All Data from TDEE Watch", or individual entries in the respective categories).
  • iCloud: you can additionally remove the app's iCloud data via iOS Settings (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → TDEE Watch).
  • Deleting the app: removing the app deletes all local app data. Data stored in Apple Health and in iCloud remains until you delete it there.

9. Withdrawing access

  • Apple Health: Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → TDEE Watch (or in the Health app under Profile → Apps). There you can revoke read and write access individually.
  • iCloud sync: turn it off in the app's settings. Data already stored in iCloud remains until you delete it (see section 8).

10. Your rights

As a data subject you have the following rights under the GDPR. Because the developer has no access to your data, you can exercise most of them directly in the app:

  • Access (Art. 15): all data the app processes is visible in the app itself. You can also export it in full using the export function.
  • Rectification (Art. 16): change profile and plan data in the app; change weight and calorie data in the app (your own entries) or in the Health app.
  • Erasure (Art. 17): "Delete all data" in the app, see section 8.
  • Data portability (Art. 20): on request the app exports your data as a JSON file in a common, machine-readable format.
  • Restriction of processing (Art. 18) and objection (Art. 21): revoke the app's Health access or turn off iCloud sync, see section 9.
  • Withdrawal of consent (Art. 7(3)): at any time, as described in section 9.
  • Complaint (Art. 77): you have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU member state of your habitual residence, place of work or the place of the alleged infringement. The German supervisory authorities are listed by the Federal Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information.

For privacy questions, contact the controller at jarno.sch.dev@proton.me. Please do not send health data by email unless it is needed to answer your question.

11. Children

TDEE Watch is not directed at children. The app is rated 4+ in the App Store because it contains no objectionable content; calorie and weight goals, however, are intended for adults. The developer does not knowingly collect data from children – as described, the developer collects no data at all.

If the app's features change, this policy will be updated. The current version is always available at this address and linked from within the app; the date of the last change appears at the top of the page.
